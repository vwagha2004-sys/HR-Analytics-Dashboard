# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview
The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze employee data and provide insights into workforce distribution, employee demographics, departmental performance, salary distribution, and monthly hiring trends.

This dashboard helps visualize key HR metrics and supports data-driven workforce analysis.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Employees:** 500
- **Total Departments:** 6
- **Average Employee Age:** 38.47 years
- **Average Salary:** ₹65.84K

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Department-wise Employee Count
- Visualizes the distribution of employees across six departments.
- Compares workforce size across IT, Operations, Sales, HR, Marketing and Finance.

### 2. Gender Distribution
- Male: 246 employees
- Female: 244 employees
- Non-disclosed: 10 employees
- Provides an overview of employee gender distribution.

### 3. Department-wise Salary Analysis
- Compares the maximum monthly salary across different departments.
- Helps visualize salary variations between departments.

### 4. Monthly Hiring Trend
- Displays the monthly employee hiring distribution.
- Helps identify variations in hiring activity throughout the year.

## Key Insights
- The organization has a workforce of 500 employees across 6 departments.
- IT has the highest employee count among the departments shown.
- Male and female employee representation is nearly equal.
- The average employee age is 38.47 years.
- The average monthly salary across employees is ₹65.84K.
- Department-wise maximum salary comparison highlights differences in salary levels across departments.
- Monthly hiring trends show variations in recruitment activity throughout the year.
